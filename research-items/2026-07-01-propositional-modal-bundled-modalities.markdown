title: 'A General Theory of Propositional Modal Bundled Modalities'
preprintURL: 'https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.26268'
publisherURL: 'https://doi.org/10.4204/EPTCS.447.17'
authorAndPubStatus: 'Joint with Yuanzhe Yang; in Advances in Modal Logic 2026'
abstract: |
  Bundled-modal logics encode a complex conceptual notion in the semantics of a single primitive modal operator. This paper develops a general theory of their expressive power and axiomatization. It gives a uniform definition of bisimulation for arbitrary bundled modalities, justified by a corresponding Hennessy–Milner property, and identifies a broad class of positive-negative-independent bundles whose logics can be axiomatized using convex neighborhood semantics and representation results. Case studies include the bundles for someone knowing, disagreement within a group, and belief without knowledge.
