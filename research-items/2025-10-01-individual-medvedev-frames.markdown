---
title: 'The Logics of Individual Medvedev Frames'
preprintURL: 'https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.20178'
publisherURL: 'https://doi.org/10.1093/jigpal/jzaf064'
authorAndPubStatus: 'Joint with Zhicheng Chen; Logic Journal of the IGPL 33(5) (2025)'
abstract: |
  For each positive integer n, the logic MLₙ is the intuitionistic logic of the Medvedev frame formed from the nonempty subsets of an n-element set. This paper characterizes n-Medvedev frames via the property of having at least n maximal points and uses the characterization to give a uniform axiomatization through a corresponding Gabbay-style rule. It also studies the compactness, disjunction property, and structural completeness of these individual logics and compares them with Medvedev's logic.
