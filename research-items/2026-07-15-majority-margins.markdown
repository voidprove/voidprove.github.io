---
title: 'Characterizations of Voting Rules Based on Majority Margins'
preprintURL: 'https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.08595'
authorAndPubStatus: 'Joint with Wesley H. Holliday and Eric Pacuit; forthcoming in Social Choice and Welfare'
abstract: |
  Margin-based (or pairwise) voting rules return the same outcome whenever two elections have the same head-to-head margins of victory and loss. This paper gives a normative characterization of that invariance for voting rules with arbitrary kinds of output. Its central axiom, Preferential Equality, requires that when two voters rank one candidate immediately above another, reversing either voter's local ranking has the same effect on the election outcome. Together with further substantively motivated axioms, this condition characterizes margin-based rules.
